## R1 Refund window
A refund can be requested within 23 days of delivery.

## R2 Damaged parcels
Damaged parcels are refunded in full when a photo is sent within 5 days of delivery.

## R3 Refund payment time
Refund money reaches the original payment method in 6 working days.

## R4 Refund limit
The assistant can issue refunds up to 50 euros. Larger refunds need approval from a staff member.
