## D1 Standard delivery
Standard delivery takes 4 working days within the country. Parcels are delivered Monday to Saturday.

## D2 Express delivery
Express delivery arrives the next working day if it is booked before 14:30. Express delivery costs 11.40 euros per parcel.

## D3 Failed delivery
After a failed delivery the driver tries twice more. The parcel then waits 9 days at the nearest depot.

## D4 Delivery to a neighbour
A driver may leave a parcel with a neighbour only if the sender allowed it. A card in the letterbox names the neighbour.
